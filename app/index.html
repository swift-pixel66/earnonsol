Liquidity has a working range.

Liquidity is placed within a selected price range. Swaps within that range can generate trading fees.

01

Stock exposure, through tokens.

Each stock vault pairs an xStock with USDC on Solana. xStocks provide stock or ETF exposure without direct shareholder rights. STONK is a community token and does not represent company shares.

02

Automatically positioned.

The vaults use Meteora DAMM V2 liquidity. A check once a day adjusts positions when the rules and available assets allow. If a price move exhausts one asset, liquidity can remain idle; automatic inventory recovery is not included. Returns are not guaranteed.

03

You hold the shares.

Redeem shares for your portion of the underlying tokens. You receive the selected asset and USDC. Withdrawal returns those assets without automatically selling the stock portion.
